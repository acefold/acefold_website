01 · The problem

Here's what we usually find.

When a brand comes to us from another agency, the same leaks show up almost every time. None of them are exotic. All of them cost you money.

×

Budget bleeding into broad match. Half the spend is chasing terms that were never going to convert, and nobody's watching the search term report.

×

No negative keyword discipline. You're paying for the same junk clicks every single day because the list never gets cleaned.

×

Campaign structure is a maze. Hundreds of campaigns, no hierarchy, impossible to read and harder to scale.

×

Bids touched once a week. The market moves every day. Weekly tweaks mean you're always a step behind the auction.

×

PPC and organic are strangers. Your ads aren't building your rank, so the second you pause spend, the sales vanish.

×

Reporting hides the truth. A clean ACOS chart looks great until you ask what's actually left after spend. Then it goes quiet.

We fix all six. Then we report against your profit, every month, with the reasoning attached.

08 · FAQ

A few things cleared up.

Do you only do PPC, or listings too?+
Both, and that's the point. The ad data tells us where the listing is leaking, and the listing work makes the ads convert. Run apart, they fight each other. Run together, they compound.
How fast do we see movement?+
Most accounts show a clear read inside the first reporting cycle, roughly two to four weeks. We build for the velocity that holds after launch, not a spike that fades by day 45.
Can we start with one ASIN?+
Yes. Most brands start with one. Once you watch the CVR and ACOS move, scaling to the rest of the catalog is an easy call.
What if we've been burned by an agency before?+
Common story. A nicer dashboard never sold anything. We rebuild the part of the funnel that's actually losing money, and we report against your profit every month so you always see what's working.
What does reporting actually look like?+
Every month: profit after spend, sales, sessions, CVR, ACOS, TACOS, what we changed, and the plan for next month. Numbers first, reasoning attached.
