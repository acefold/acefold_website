06 · FAQ

A few things cleared up.

Do you only do PPC, or listings too?+
Both, and that's the point. The PPC data tells us where the listing is leaking, and the listing work makes the PPC convert. Run separately, they fight each other. Run together, they compound.
How fast do we see movement?+
Most accounts show a read inside the first reporting cycle, roughly two to four weeks. We build for the velocity that holds after launch, not a spike that fades by day 45.

Yes. Most brands start with one. Once you see the CVR and ACOS move, scaling to the rest of the catalog is an easy call.
