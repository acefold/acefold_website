FAQ

Amazon PPC agency questions.

What does an Amazon PPC agency actually do?+
We run your full Amazon presence: listing SEO, PPC across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display and DSP, product launches, and monthly profit reporting. One team, judged against your bottom line rather than billable hours.
Do you only do Amazon PPC, or listings too?+
Both, and that is the point. Ad data shows where the listing is leaking, and listing work makes the ads convert. Run apart they fight each other. Run together they compound.
How do I get started with Acefold?+
Book a free 90 minute Amazon account audit. We review your campaigns and listings, show you where ad spend is leaking, and you decide if it is a fit. No deck, no pressure.
Do you work with new or small Amazon brands?+
Yes. Most brands start with a single ASIN. Once you watch the CVR and ACOS move, scaling to the rest of the catalog is an easy call.
How fast do Amazon PPC results show up?+
Most accounts show a clear read inside the first reporting cycle, roughly two to four weeks. We build for velocity that holds, not a spike that fades.
What does your Amazon reporting look like?+
Every month: profit after spend, sales, sessions, CVR, ACOS, TACOS, what we changed, why it moved, and the plan for next month. Numbers first, reasoning attached.
What if we have been burned by an Amazon agency before?+
Common story. A nicer dashboard never sold anything. We rebuild the part of the funnel that's actually losing money and report against your profit so you always see what's working.
How is Acefold priced?+
We tie our incentives to your growth, not billable hours. We'll walk you through the structure on the audit call once we understand your account.
